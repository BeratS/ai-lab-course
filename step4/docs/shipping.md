# Shipping

Studio Lumi is a fictional dress shop based in Skopje. We ship every order from our workshop in Skopje.

## North Macedonia

Delivery within North Macedonia takes 2 to 3 working days. Shipping costs 150 MKD. Orders over 3,000 MKD ship for free.

## Kosovo and Albania

Delivery to Kosovo and Albania takes 4 to 6 working days. Shipping costs 400 MKD for any order size.

## European Union

Delivery to EU countries takes 7 to 10 working days. Shipping costs 15 EUR. Customs fees, if any, are paid by the customer.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email on the day it is shipped. Orders placed after 14:00 are shipped the next working day.

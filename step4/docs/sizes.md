# Sizes and custom tailoring

## Size chart (in cm)

| Size | Bust | Waist | Hips |
|------|------|-------|------|
| XS   | 80   | 62    | 88   |
| S    | 84   | 66    | 92   |
| M    | 88   | 70    | 96   |
| L    | 94   | 76    | 102  |
| XL   | 100  | 82    | 108  |

If you are between two sizes, we recommend the larger size.

## Custom tailoring

Any dress can be tailored to your exact measurements. Custom tailoring costs an extra 1,500 MKD and adds 7 working days to the delivery time.

To order a tailored dress, send your bust, waist, hip and height measurements in the order notes.
